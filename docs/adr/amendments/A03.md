# A03 — Near-duplicate divergence detector

> **PENDING SOURCE.** Built only from the owner's summary (2026-10-01). The addendum wins where it differs.

## What v2 said

**The old pipeline excluded near-duplicates from contradiction checking.**

## The conceded flaw

Excluding them **dropped the most dangerous case**: pairs that are *mostly* identical and differ in exactly the fact that
matters. This is the same mistake the Phase −1 brief lists as "near-duplicate detection that drops the most divergent pair".

## What the amendment changes

Add a **near-duplicate divergence detector**: find pairs that are *mostly* identical and **surface exactly where they differ.**
It is the **highest-precision LLM-free class** in the pipeline.

### Required shape (owner's instruction for the Spike 3 proof of concept)

The divergence check must:

1. compare the **changed spans** of the updated page against an **"approved sources"** set, and
2. **not drop near-identical pairs**, which means no upper similarity cut-off and no top-k truncation that could remove the
   highest-severity pair.

Spike 3 reports both quotes and **character offsets**, not just a match
([`../../spikes/03-change-time-trigger-poc.md`](../../spikes/03-change-time-trigger-poc.md)).
